About Me

I have a background in science, art, and technology. I've always been interested in learning new things, which has led me to explore different fields.

I studied Media Arts and Design at Middlesex College, where I focused on 3D modeling, animation, and game development. I'm currently studying Graphic Information Technology at Arizona State University, with a focus on Full-Stack Web Development.

I'm interested in combining my programming and artistic skills, especially in areas like real-time graphics, shaders, and game development. My goal is to become a Technical Artist.

Outside of school, I enjoy traveling, scuba diving, and working on personal projects.
